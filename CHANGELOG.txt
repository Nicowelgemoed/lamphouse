Lamphouse / Answer Print — changes to the spektrafilm film profiles
==================================================================

The spektrafilm licence asks that modifications to its profiles be described in a
CHANGELOG.txt shipped with SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt. This file does that.

How the data was modified
-------------------------
1. Missing samples (NaN) in log spectral sensitivity, spectral dye density and base density were
   filled by linear interpolation, holding the nearest valid value at the ends of the range.
2. Spectral data was resampled from 380-780 nm at 5 nm to 380-740 nm at 10 nm (37 samples) for the
   DCTL. Measured effect on the final image: at most 0.22 ΔE2000 against the 5 nm data.
3. Characteristic (density) curves were resampled from 256 samples to 64 uniform samples over
   log exposure -3 to +4, for Catmull-Rom interpolation in the DCTL (at most 0.004 density error).
4. Derived quantities computed from the profiles and stored in the DCTLs, which are encodings of
   the profile information and therefore also CC BY-SA 4.0:
   - per negative: a second-order homogeneous polynomial mapping DaVinci Wide Gamut to layer
     exposures, fitted by integrating the profile's spectral sensitivities against ~1,500 surface
     spectra under the stock's rated illuminant (3200 K or D55);
   - per negative: mid-scale gamma of each layer's curve (used by the interlayer model);
   - per print: printer-band x print-layer exposure weights (3200 K tungsten lamp, Schott KG3 heat
     filter, generic additive-printer dichroic bands at 492 / 588 nm);
   - per print: projection-to-XYZ weights (Kinoton 75P xenon lamp, CIE 1931 2° observer,
     normalised so clear print base = Y 1.0, Bradford-adapted to D65);
   - per negative/print pair: printer gains that print an 18% grey neutral at visual density 1.0,
     and the print log exposure of that grey (pivot for the print contrast control).
5. Nothing in the profiles was re-measured or edited by hand.

Other inputs not from spektrafilm
---------------------------------
  - Schott KG3 heat filter transmission: distributed with spektrafilm (data/filters), used as is.
  - CIE colour matching functions, CIE D55/D65 and Kinoton 75P spectra: colour-science library.
  - Grain amount per stock, halation strength, interlayer default and bleach-bypass silver density
    are Lamphouse's own settings, not from spektrafilm or Kodak (see docs/how-it-works.md).
